Background

A builder at heart

I am a software developer based in Yola, Adamawa State, with a strong interest in civic technology, environmental data and products that work well on low-bandwidth connections.

I started the Yola AI Info Hub to give residents one reliable place for local environmental intelligence — not just raw data, but actionable guidance they can use every day.

When I am not coding, I am usually learning about mentoring early-stage developers or exploring how AI can be made practical for Nigerian communities.
