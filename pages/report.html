Why report

Help us keep Yola accurate

Yola AI Info Hub is powered by the community. If something looks wrong, out of date, or missing entirely, your report helps us fix it fast — for you and for everyone else searching for the same answer.

Reports are reviewed by our local team. You do not need an account, and personal details are optional unless you want a reply.

The process

How it works

1. Submit

Fill in the form below with as much detail as you can — the more we know, the faster we can act.

2. Review

Our local team reviews every report and cross-checks it with trusted sources here in Yola.

3. Update

We update the hub, and if you left your email, we let you know what changed.
