What you can do here

Six tools, one environment hub

Everything works on a phone, over a weak connection, and keeps your data on your device unless you choose to submit it.

Atmospheric conditions

Temperature now and hour-by-hour for 24 hours, wind, gusts, humidity, UV, pressure, rainfall and a 7-day outlook.

Air quality & clarity

PM2.5, PM10, Saharan dust, ozone and a driving-clarity score that tells you when visibility is risky.

AI waste classifier

Snap a photo or describe an item — get recyclable / hazardous status and safe disposal steps for Yola.

Illegal dumping reports

Photo evidence, GPS pin, severity and a tracking code so you can follow the clean-up to completion.

Recycling & drop-off map

Buy-back hubs, e-waste intake, composting and hazardous waste points sorted by distance from you.

Collection schedule

Pickup days by ward, calendar export, reminders, and a household waste log to track your recycling rate.
