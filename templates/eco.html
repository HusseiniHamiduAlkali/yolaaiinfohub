Golden rules

Four habits that make every classification easier

Clean and dry

Rinse containers. Food residue contaminates whole batches of recyclables and kills their value.

Separate materials

Remove metal lids from glass, caps from bottles, batteries from devices before drop-off.

Never burn

Burning plastics, nylon and e-waste releases dioxins and heavy metals into the air you breathe.

Isolate hazards

Medicines, sharps, paints, pesticides and batteries always go to the specialist intake point.

Prepare before you go

Sort by material

Mixed bags get rejected or downgraded. Keep PET, nylon, metal, paper and glass in separate sacks.

Rinse and dry

Clean material weighs less and sells for more. Wet cardboard is usually refused outright.

Bulk up first

Accumulate a few kilograms before a trip — one journey with 10 kg beats five with 2 kg.
