About the Hospital

Modibbo Adama Specialist Hospital is the premier secondary referral facility in Adamawa State, providing comprehensive specialist medical care to the Yola metropolitan area and surrounding communities. Established in 1995, the hospital serves as a critical bridge between primary healthcare centres and the state teaching hospital.

The hospital serves over 200,000 patients annually, offering 24-hour emergency services, specialist outpatient clinics, advanced diagnostic services, and surgical care across multiple disciplines.

Our History

Founded in 1995 as a regional medical centre, the hospital was upgraded to specialist status in 2012 following major infrastructure investment. Today it serves as a teaching affiliate and a model for secondary care delivery in northeastern Nigeria.
